Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored? Is it private?

On your device. If you turn on iCloud sync it is also stored in your own iCloud account (CloudKit private database). The developer never receives your data — see the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn iCloud sync on or off?

Settings → Sync → iCloud sync. Both devices must be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive enabled. Restart the app after changing the setting.

Calendar events or Reminders don’t show up

Grant access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars / Reminders → Daily Page (choose Full Access). Then pull down on the Today page to refresh.

Voice memos are not transcribed

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. On-device recognition needs the language’s dictation data — enable Dictation in Settings → General → Keyboard.

Widgets show old data

Open the app once (widgets refresh after each change), or remove and re-add the widget. Live Activities appear only while a focus session is running.

How do I export or back up everything?

Settings → Data → Export (Markdown / PDF) or JSON backup. The JSON file can be re-imported on another device via Settings → Data → Import.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings → Data → Erase all data. To remove the iCloud copy too, turn iCloud sync off first, then delete the app’s data under iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage → Daily Page.

Daily Page Pro, subscriptions and refunds

Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly, 7-day free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase, billed by Apple. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Use Restore Purchases in the paywall after reinstalling. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Face ID lock: I can’t open the app

The app falls back to your device passcode when Face ID fails. If biometrics are disabled for the app in iOS Settings, re-enable them under Settings → Face ID & Passcode → Other Apps.
